# BitLink Monetization Strategy

## 1. Freemium Plans

Most SaaS freemium products convert 2–5% of free users into paying customers. Start simple and optimize later.

| Plan | Price | Key Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Basic shortening, 30 links, 7-day analytics |
| Pro | $9/mo | Unlimited links, passwords, expiration, 1-year analytics |
| Business | $29/mo | Teams, API, advanced analytics |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, audit logs, dedicated support |

Target: 3% free-to-paid conversion.

---

## 2. Ads on Redirect Pages

Show a short ad page before redirecting users.

- 5-second interstitial ads
- Best for social and Telegram traffic
- Average CPM: $2–$6
- Tier-1 traffic (US, UK, CA) can reach $8+ CPM

Example:
500,000 monthly redirects × $4 CPM = ~$2,000/month

---

## 4. Premium Analytics

Free users get:

- Total clicks
- Last 7 days

Premium ($5/mo):

- Country and city data
- Device and browser stats
- Referrer tracking
- CSV export
- UTM analytics
